# ModCrypt name and branding

"ModCrypt", the ModCrypt crypt logo, and the Toxic Crypt look of the app (its color scheme, layout and artwork) identify the official ModCrypt made by Josh (joshcarterky).

- Do not use the ModCrypt name or logo for another app, fork, website, mod pack or service.
- Do not publish software that looks like ModCrypt or could be mistaken for it.
- Mod pack authors **may** say their pack "works with ModCrypt" or is "made for ModCrypt", as long as the pack's own name is different and it does not use the logo.

The only official downloads are at https://github.com/joshcarterky/ModCrypt/releases. Copies from anywhere else are unofficial and may be unsafe.
